Privacy Policy

JQL Activity Stream · Last updated 30 August 2026

Summary

JQL Activity Stream stores no data. It has no database, no file storage and no servers of its own, and it sends nothing to anyone. Each time the gadget is displayed it asks Jira for information, shows it, and forgets it.

What the app accesses

When you view the gadget, it reads from your own Jira site:

  • the issues matched by the JQL query or saved filter you configured, with their keys and summaries;
  • the change history of those issues — which field changed, when, and the display name of the person who changed it.

Nothing else. The app requests a single permission, read:jira-work, and no write permission of any kind.

Whose permissions apply

Every request is made as the person viewing the gadget, never as the app. Jira's own permissions apply unchanged, so the gadget cannot show anyone an issue they could not already open themselves.

What is stored

Nothing. The app declares no storage in its manifest — no Forge Key-Value Store, no Forge SQL, no external database, no logs of your data. Information fetched from Jira exists only for the moment it takes to render the gadget.

The one thing that persists is the gadget's own configuration — your JQL string, time window, event types and item limit. That is held by Atlassian as part of your dashboard, not by us.

What is sent elsewhere

Nothing. The app makes no network requests to any host outside Atlassian. There is no analytics, no tracking and no third-party service. Because it runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform, your data never leaves Atlassian's infrastructure.

Sub-processors

None, because no data is transferred anywhere.

Retention and deletion

There is no retained data to delete. Uninstalling the app removes its gadget configuration with it. Written confirmation is available on request at the address below.

Children

The app is a workplace tool sold to organisations and is not directed at children.
