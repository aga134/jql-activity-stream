Privacy Policy

Page Tree Sort · Last updated 1 September 2026

Summary

The app stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It reads the titles of the child pages you ask it to sort, works out the order, and reorders those pages inside your own Confluence site. Nothing leaves Atlassian’s infrastructure at any point.

What the app reads

When you open Sort child pages A–Z on a page, it reads the titles and page ids of that page’s direct children, and nothing else. It does not read page bodies, comments, attachments, labels, or anything about the people who wrote them.

What the app changes

Only the order of those child pages, and only when you press the sort button. It does not create, edit, rename, re-parent, archive or delete anything. Page content is never touched.

What the app stores

Nothing. There is no database, no Forge storage, no cache and no log of its own. Every use reads the current state from Confluence and discards it when the dialog closes. There is nothing to export, nothing to delete on request, and nothing that survives uninstalling the app.

Where processing happens

Entirely on Atlassian’s Forge platform and in your browser. The app makes no request to any host outside Atlassian, uses no external service, runs no containers, and makes no AI or machine-learning calls of any kind on your data.

Whose permissions apply

Every request to Confluence is made as you, never as the app. Confluence’s own permission model applies unchanged: the app can only see pages you can see and only move pages you could move by hand. A page restricted from you is not in the order the app computes and cannot be moved by it — the result is correct for the pages you can see and untouched for any you cannot.

Personal data

The app processes page titles, which your team writes and which may incidentally contain a person’s name. It does not read author names, account ids or profile data, and stores none of it. Under the GDPR the customer remains the data controller for everything in their Confluence site.

Sub-processors

None. There are no third parties, because no data reaches any.

Retention and deletion

Nothing is retained, so there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling removes the app completely and leaves your pages exactly as they were at that moment.
